This opens the site in a fresh tab that is already in this session, so you sign in normally and this session remembers only that account. The account you use anywhere else is not touched. This is the safe way to add a Google account: sign in fresh rather than copying one in.

Backup

Saves this session's cookies to a file, or restores one. A backup holds live logins, so keep it safe. A device-bound login (a modern Google account) will not work on another machine even from a backup.

Adopt what you are signed into

Everything the browser is already carrying, read from this profile. Cookies are copied, never moved, so the tabs you have open keep working exactly as they do now.

For an account you also keep using in your normal browser, Google most of all, Sign in here on a session is safer than adopting: it signs in fresh instead of copying a login that a big provider can read as stolen. See the guide.
